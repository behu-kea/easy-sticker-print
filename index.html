THE SHORT VERSION

Your next print, in four steps.

  1. Choose your image. Transparent PNGs give the best contour. For a white-background JPEG, choose solid background removal.
  2. Set the finished width. The height follows automatically. Leave the border at 0 to follow the artwork, or use −0.75 mm for a small inset.
  3. Inspect every pink path. Separate artwork islands produce separate stickers. Adjust the border to join nearby islands, or use a rectangle / ellipse. Interior holes are off by default.
  4. Export EPS or PDF. Import into VersaWorks, confirm the recognized cut contour, select Print & Cut, and set copies there. Keep the job at 100% scale.

These files contain a real vector CutContour spot-color stroke. The guide specifies 0.25 mm; a 0.25 pt option is also available.

Artwork exports in RGB; VersaWorks controls the print conversion. Transparent areas and borders assume white media. This app does not generate a white-ink channel for clear material. Test a single sticker before a production run.

Outlines are traced at up to 1,400 pixels on the longest side. Original artwork resolution is retained for export, up to 24 megapixels. Tiny details and very large stickers need closer inspection.
